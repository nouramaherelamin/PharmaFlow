Model Info

How the two models were built and how well they really perform.

Honest result. In this dataset each medicine sells almost the same amount every month, and nothing in the data (supplier, price, month, branch) explains the differences. So the medicine-level model can do no better than predicting the average (R² ≈ 0) and gives a wide range instead of a falsely precise number. The category-level revenue model scores much higher because categories differ in size (e.g. 22 antibiotics vs 2 wellness products).

About

PharmaFlow is an end-to-end machine-learning project on pharmacy sales data.

What it does

  • Predict Sales: expected units and revenue for a medicine at a branch next month, with a range and a stock check.
  • Forecast: next-month revenue per branch and category.
  • Analytics: KPIs, trends and heatmaps.

The data

50,000 transactions from 7 branches, 148 medicines in 15 categories, January 2022 to June 2023. Forecasts are for the month after the last data month.

Method

  • Cleaning and feature engineering (lags, rolling means, calendar, medicine attributes).
  • Time-based train/test split, no shuffling.
  • Ridge, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting compared against simple baselines.
  • Flask API + Chart.js interface.

Limits

  • Predictions are statistical expectations, not guarantees.
  • Price changes the revenue estimate but not the unit forecast (no price effect is visible in this data).
  • Only one month ahead is forecast.
